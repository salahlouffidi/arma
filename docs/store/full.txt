ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION is a fast arcade survival game: roll your armadillo across beautiful 3D tabletop arenas, collect fruit and escape hungry snakes that grow longer every time you feed.

NEW IN VERSION 2.1
• 20 rounds, including the new World 3: Neon City, Deep Ocean, Candy Land and Cosmos
• Power-ups: MAGNET pulls fruit to you, FREEZE slows the snakes, x2 POINTS doubles your score, SNAKE SHRINK cuts every snake down to size
• Combos up to x6 when you chain fruit quickly
• 5 kinds of fruit worth different points: apple, orange, grapes, cherries and watermelon
• Surprise events: Fruit Rain, Snake Frenzy and the Mystery Box jackpot
• Brand-new graphics: lit wooden boards, animated themes, glowing snakes, fruit pops and floating scores
• Vibration feedback for special fruit, power-ups and combos
• Redesigned menus, pause screen and round selection

GAME MODES
• Rounds 1 to 8: single-armadillo missions
• Dual-armadillo rounds: control two armadillos at once with two thumbs
• OVERDRIVE star fruit: speed, agility and a shield that absorbs one snake strike

FEATURES
• Smooth virtual joystick controls designed for landscape play
• Progressive difficulty with more snakes, moving boulders, lava and escaping fruit
• High scores for every round
• Plays offline
